Great sound, but not perfect

I have been using these wireless headphones for about three weeks now, mainly for commuting and working from home.

The sound quality is excellent for the price. The bass is rich without being overwhelming, and voices come through clearly, which makes them great for podcasts. The noise cancellation works well on the train, although it struggles a little with wind noise outdoors.

Battery life is impressive: I only charge them once a week. They are also comfortable for long sessions, even if they get slightly warm after a couple of hours.

My main complaint is the companion app, which is confusing and sometimes fails to connect. The touch controls are also a bit too sensitive, and I often pause my music by accident when adjusting them.

Overall, I would recommend them to anyone looking for good value, but be prepared for a few small annoyances.

Rating: 4 out of 5.
