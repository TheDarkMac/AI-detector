Five simple habits that boosted my productivity

For years, I felt busy all the time but rarely finished what mattered. Here are five small changes that made a real difference.

1. I plan tomorrow tonight. Spending ten minutes each evening writing down my three priorities means I start the day with a clear focus.

2. I work in focused blocks. Using a timer, I work for 45 minutes without checking my phone, then take a short break.

3. I batch my emails. Instead of answering messages as they arrive, I check my inbox twice a day.

4. I say no more often. Not every meeting needs my presence, and declining politely frees up hours each week.

5. I protect my sleep. It sounds obvious, but going to bed at a regular time has done more for my concentration than any app.

None of these habits are revolutionary, but together they have helped me feel calmer and get more done.
