Incident report

Date of incident: Tuesday morning, approximately 9:40 a.m.
Location: Warehouse B, loading bay 3

Description: While a delivery truck was being unloaded, a pallet of boxed equipment slipped from the forklift and fell to the ground. The forklift operator immediately stopped the vehicle and secured the area. No one was injured.

Cause: Initial checks suggest that the pallet was damaged and had not been properly inspected before loading. The forklift was operating within normal speed limits.

Damage: Four boxes of equipment were damaged. The estimated cost of replacement is still being assessed.

Actions taken: The area was cleared and the remaining pallets were inspected. The supplier has been informed of the damaged pallet.

Recommendations: Reinforce the inspection procedure for incoming pallets and provide a refresher training session for forklift operators.

Report prepared by the shift supervisor.
