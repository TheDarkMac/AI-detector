Climate change is one of the most pressing challenges of our time. Over the past century, human activities, particularly the burning of fossil fuels, have significantly increased the concentration of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere. As a result, global temperatures have risen, leading to more frequent heatwaves, melting glaciers and rising sea levels.

The consequences are not only environmental but also social and economic. Droughts threaten food security in many regions, while extreme weather events cause costly damage to homes and infrastructure. The most vulnerable populations, who have contributed the least to the problem, are often the most affected.

Addressing climate change requires action at every level. Governments must invest in renewable energy and set ambitious emission targets. Businesses need to rethink their supply chains, and individuals can make a difference through their choices in transport, diet and consumption.

Although the task is immense, it is not hopeless. With cooperation and determination, a more sustainable future remains within reach.
